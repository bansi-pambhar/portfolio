About

I'm a DevOps and platform engineer who builds and operates cloud-native systems: containerized deployments on Docker and Kubernetes, GitOps delivery with ArgoCD, CI/CD pipelines with Jenkins, and infrastructure automation with Terraform and Ansible across AWS, Azure, and GCP. I bring a security-first lens to this work — DevSecOps, SAST/DAST, container and Kubernetes security, GitOps policy enforcement, and vulnerability assessment and penetration testing (VAPT) — so the platforms I build are hardened by design, not patched after the fact.
